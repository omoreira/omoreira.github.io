The Open Skies Library

Where curiosity meets compassion—and every learner can find a place to belong

About the Project

The Open Skies Library is a digital platform designed to make science accessible, emotionally supportive, and culturally inclusive. It curates and annotates open-access research, educational media, and historical manuscripts—while honoring Indigenous sky knowledge, spotlighting women in STEM, and empowering learners historically excluded from science.

Critical thinking and the scientific method are not privileges—they are rights. This project helps ensure everyone can explore, question, and understand the world around them.

Core Features

  • Annotated open-access scientific literature
  • Educational content and historical texts
  • Curated learning paths and self-paced modules
  • Spotlights on marginalized scientists and cultural sky stories
  • Legal compliance under Canadian fair dealing laws
  • Supportive tools for learners recovering from academic burnout or exclusion
