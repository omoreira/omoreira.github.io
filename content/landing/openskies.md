The **OM OpenSkies Initiative** makes science accessible, emotionally supportive, and culturally inclusive. Bridging **rigorous research** with human experience. The platform curates **open-access literature, educational media, and historical texts**, while honoring **Indigenous sky knowledge** and spotlighting **women in STEM.**

Core strands:
* the Library,
* Atlas of the Skies,
* Voices in Science,
* and the SkyMemory Archive.

**We're seeking seed support, fiscal hosting, and collaborator-advisors to bring the pilot to life.**
