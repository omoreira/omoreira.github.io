About Me

I build humane systems at the edge of science and AI.

Hello! I'm Olga Moreira.

I hold a PhD and MSc in Astrophysics from the University of Liège, and a BSc and MSc in Physics and Applied Mathematics (Astronomy) from the University of Porto.

Based in the Niagara region, I now focus on auditable, local-first, modular, and decentralized AI and knowledge systems, as well as inclusive science outreach that amplifies underrepresented perspectives. My work centers on building technologies that are open, accessible, and sustainable, prioritizing user autonomy, resource efficiency, and reduced reliance on energy-intensive infrastructure.

Rooted in collaborations at the European Space Research and Technology Centre (ESTEC), the European Southern Observatory (ESO), and confidential consultancy roles, my career has evolved in parallel with the active deployment of my organization, OM Synaptic Systems.On the education side, I currently contribute to the undergraduate courses “Introduction to Physics", “Introduction to Astronomy,” and “Foundations of Natural Science” at Newlane University.

AI Integration & Systems Analysis

For me, decentralization and modularity are more than engineering choices: they're a path towards accessibility, portability, privacy, and a lighter environmental footprint.

As founder of OM Synaptic Systems, I help nonprofits, small businesses, and academic teams design AI-assisted systems that prioritize structural integrity, ethical alignment, and human-centered outcomes by avoiding proprietary "black box" systems to make AI workflows auditable and interpretable, and by focusing on scalable solutions that address root challenges in science, education, and organizational resilience.

OM Synaptic Systems' Open Science Commitment is built on a multidisciplinary approach that ensures AI systems are robust, ethical, and aligned with the principles of transparency and equity. Below, we outline our services, R&D actions.

Consultancy Services

  • System Audits: Use the Five-Lens Framework to evaluate existing AI systems for structural integrity and ethical alignment. Identify gaps and provide actionable recommendations for improvement.
  • Workshops: Train stakeholders on integrating the Five-Lens approach into development pipelines. Offer "Ethics-by-Design" sprints to embed principles of transparency, fairness, and accountability early in the design process.
  • Toolkits: Develop modular, open-source resources to support practitioners. Examples include checklists for algorithmic fairness, templates for epistemic transparency, and guides for decentralized data practices.

Research &Developement Actions

  • Proof-of-Concept: We work at the backend of decentralized AI platform for initiatives that prioritizes accessibility and equity.
  • Collaborations: Partner with academia and industry to publish case studies on decentralized data practices, equity-driven AI governance, and systemic integrity in AI development.
  • Ongoing Research: Explore cutting-edge applications of the Five-Lens Framework, such as adaptive algorithms for marginalized communities or frameworks for auditing AI in high-stakes domains like healthcare and criminal justice.

By combining rigorous analysis with a commitment to equity and transparency, we empower organizations to build AI systems that are not only technically sound but also socially responsible.

Open Science Engagement

The scientific method and critical thinking should be harnessed as instruments of collective liberation, not hoarded as means of exclusion by those who weaponize knowledge to uphold elitist hierarchies.

As a researcher and open science advocate, I prioritize making science accessible to all. I collaborate with educators, developers, and communities to design tools and practices that democratize scientific knowledge, ensuring it serves as a shared resource rather than a gatekept privilege.

Core Commitments:

I align my work with the principles set out by the TU/e Code of Scientific Conduct and IGDORE's Open Scientific Practices, integrating them into every stage of my research, writing, and project design.

  • Integrity and Transparency — Upholding clarity in data, sources, and authorship.
  • Openness and Accessibility — Sharing knowledge responsibly, making science understandable and available to all.
  • Equity and Responsibility — Recognizing the social impact of research and prioritizing inclusivity in scientific participation.
  • Reflection and Accountability — Continuously examining how methods, technologies, and biases shape the stories we tell through science.

Non-traditional Educational Approaches

My approach to education emphasizes accessibility, inclusivity, and the integration of diverse perspectives. I believe that science should be a shared endeavor, and I strive to create learning experiences that reflect this value.

Astrophysics & Space Science Outreach

I integrate visual abstraction techniques, develop tactile astronomy tools, and co-create curricula with neurodivergent communities to make cosmic exploration accessible to all learners.

OM OpenSkies Initiative

I'm developing the OM OpenSkies Initiative, a long-term project to create a global platform for open-access astrophysics research, modern science education, and inclusive storytelling. It will create emotionally supportive, culturally inclusive scientific resources and integrate Indigenous sky traditions and marginalized voices into a shared vision of the cosmos. Planned projects include the OM Atlas of the Skies, Silenced Voices in Science, and the SkyMemory Archive. The initiative is grounded in the idea that science is a shared right, not a privilege.

Solar Physics Research

My doctoral research explored the seismic rhythms of red giants and the precision of interferometry. However, it was my early studies in astro-MHD that first ignited my passion for astrophysics. While I no longer focus on asteroseismology, analytical and computational tools I developed continue to inform my independent research on solar dynamics, flare statistics, and the interplay between magnetic activity in the Sun and other stars. Today, I'm particularly excited about integrating MHD simulations with machine learning to advance data-driven heliophysics, a field that bridges the elegance of classical plasma physics with the transformative potential of modern AI.
