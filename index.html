About Me

Hi! I'm Olga Moreira, an astrophysicist and AI knowledge architect. I build humane, modular systems where starlight meets code and compassion steers the method. My work ranges from asteroseismology and solar physics to semantic systems for machine-learning knowledge bases. I hold a PhD from the University of Liege and a BSc in Physics & Applied Mathematics (Astronomy) from the University of Porto, and I’ve worked with ESA and ESO. Based in the Niagara region, I design instruction-driven, auditable AI workflows and science-communication projects.

My compass: intellectual honesty, openness, independence, societal responsibility, and epistemic humility.
Science is a method; ethics is our choice.

Feel free to explore my site to learn more about my research, projects, and writing.

    OM OpenSkies Initiative

    The OM OpenSkies Initiative makes science accessible, emotionally supportive, and culturally inclusive. Bridging rigorous research with human experience. The platform curates open-access literature, educational media, and historical texts, while honoring Indigenous sky knowledge and spotlighting women in STEM.

    Core strands: the Library, Atlas of the Skies, Voices in Science, and the SkyMemory Archive.

    We're seeking seed support, fiscal hosting, and collaborator-advisors to bring the pilot to life.

    Sam AI

    Sam AI is a trauma-informed, modular, portable assistant built around local-first execution, modular tools, and portable contextual memory. It runs in chat for dialogue, and as an agent for multi-step tasks—so you can research, plan, and truth-check thought distortions without surrendering your data. The aim isn’t to replace judgment; it’s to scaffold clarity and momentum with auditable, humane automation.

    Current milestones: core architecture and portable memory are working; an open-core release and mobile UI are on the roadmap.

    Creative Writing

    I write essays and stories from the bright edge of recovery, mapping where science, memory, and meaning meet. Under my pen names (Mia Graves; Doctor OM-some), I explore courage, multilingual identity, and the strange comedy of being human after hard things.

    Series in development: Mia Graves' Memoirs (a trilogy), “Unsung Women in Science,” “Gold, Ivory, Spices, and Slaves,” and “Code-Switchers.”

    These pieces are not therapy notes. They're craft. Raw, lyrical, and logically grounded. Meant to share a life experience and maybe, in the process, to help others feel less alone while keeping the scientific method in view.
