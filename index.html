About Me

Hi! I'm Olga Moreira, an astrophysicist and AI knowledge architect.

I design decentralized, portable, modular systems. My work bridges astrophysics with semantic systems for machine-learning knowledge bases.

I hold a PhD + MSc in Astrophysicsfrom the University of Liege and a BSc + MSc in Physics & Applied Mathematics (Astronomy) from the University of Porto. I've worked with both the European Space Agency (ESA) and the European Southern Observatory (ESO). Based in the Niagara region, I now focus on instruction-driven, auditable AI workflows and science-communication projects that unite logic, openness, diversity, and inclusivity.

My philosophy is rooted in local-first, eco-conscious design. Systems that run on user devices, adapt to low-resource settings, and reduce dependence on energy-intensive centralized infrastructure. For me, decentralization and modularity are more than engineering choices: they're a path towards accessibility, portability, privacy, and a lighter environmental footprint.

Data Visualization Tools

My passion for data visualization began early in my research career, when I discovered that beauty and clarity could coexist in science. Whether modeling stellar interiors or visualizing magnetic flows, I've always sought ways to make complexity intuitive, transforming numbers and equations into motion, structure, and light.

Throughout my work in astrophysics and AI knowledge systems, I have created scientific and educational animations based on both synthetic simulations and real observational data, helping audiences grasp what equations alone could not.

Sam AI

Sam AI is a trauma-informed, modular, and portable personal assistant built around local-first execution, modular tools, and contextual memory you control. It runs in chat for dialogue and as an agent for multi-step tasks: so you can research, plan, and truth-check thought distortions without surrendering your data.

The goal is NOT to replace your therapist. It's to extend the tools of reflection and logic into everyday life. To support clarity and momentum through auditable, humane automation.

  • Current milestones: core architecture and portable memory are functional; an open-core release and mobile UI are on the roadmap.

Keep it private. Keep it local. Tune Sam into becoming your companion, not your overlord.

Creative Writing

I write essays and stories from the bright edge of recovery, mapping where science, memory, and meaning meet. Under my pen names (Mia Graves; Doctor OM-some), I explore courage, multilingual identity, and the strange comedy of being human after hard things.

  • Series in development: Mia Graves' Memoirs (a trilogy) and a collection of essays exploring science, identity, and ethics, including Unsung Women in Science; Gold, Ivory, Spices, and Slaves; AI: To Explore or to Exploit?; Logical Fallacies and the Scientific Method; and Code-Switchers.

These pieces are not therapy notes. They're craft. Raw, lyrical, and logically grounded. Meant to share a life experience and maybe, in the process, to help others feel less alone while keeping the scientific method in view.
